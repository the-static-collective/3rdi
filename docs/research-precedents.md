## Formal neighbors

### Bitemporal data

Temporal database work distinguishes valid time from transaction time. That is a strong boring neighbor for `occurred_at` versus `available_from`, although `3rdi` adds observer/audience exposure and attention as separate project-specific coordinates. See Richard Snodgrass's [temporal data case study](https://cs.ulb.ac.be/public/_media/teaching/infoh415/teradata_temporal_case_study.pdf) and Kvet, Krsak, and Matiasko's discussion of unitemporal and bitemporal state histories ([DOI 10.1002/cpe.5209](https://doi.org/10.1002/cpe.5209)).

### Provenance

The W3C [PROV Data Model](https://www.w3.org/TR/prov-dm/) distinguishes entities, activities, agents, and their provenance relations. `3rdi` borrows the discipline of explicit production and attribution, not the complete PROV ontology.

Visualization provenance research separates data, interaction, insight, and rationale provenance and emphasizes recall, replication, recovery, collaboration, and analysis. This supports recording a projection's formation rather than treating the rendered view as self-authenticating. See Xu et al., 2020 ([DOI 10.1111/cgf.14035](https://doi.org/10.1111/cgf.14035)).

### Hindsight

Hindsight research shows that knowing an outcome can inflate its apparent predictability relative to foresight. The project inference is that a strict knowledge horizon is a useful engineering control; the research does not validate the complete `3rdi` model. See Hom and Van Nuland, 2019 ([DOI 10.1002/acp.3519](https://doi.org/10.1002/acp.3519)).

### Coordinate operations

PROJ's operation selection explicitly considers source/target CRS, area of interest or use, available grids, and desired accuracy. That supports requiring those fields in a location decoder receipt and refusing to imply that coordinates equal a locus. See the official [coordinate-operation computation documentation](https://proj.org/en/stable/operations/operations_computation.html).

## Deliberate non-promotion

None of these precedents proves a universal third-eye organ, theological gate algebra, mercy topology, seed metaphysics, or a privileged model of time. They show that the v0 distinctions have formal and engineering neighbors strong enough to support executable controls.
