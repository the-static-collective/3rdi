# Hatch Labs

## `TEMPORAL-COORDINATE-001`

Five immutable occurrences and three cuts.

Pass conditions:

- occurrence bytes remain stable across cuts;
- focus changes derived temporal role;
- availability changes observer visibility;
- historical mode rejects `known_at > focus_at`;
- actual future occurrences stay withheld;
- a prior expectation can remain visible as anticipated future;
- shuffled chronology with unchanged labels changes or invalidates the field.

## `CAUSAL-RELEVANCE-001`

Freeze the causal ledger, then add a later relevance edge.

Pass conditions:

- causal ledger digest remains byte-stable;
- relevance ledger digest changes;
- the new edge retains its own birthday, assessment history, and evidence;
- no later citation or interpretive use is promoted into earlier influence.

## `GLYPH-RECEIVER-001`

Decode carrier `022100` under:

- `EADGBE / 12-TET / A440`;
- `CGCEGD / 12-TET / A444`.

The decoder must declare `temperament: 12-TET`; the lab refuses an undeclared or different temperament rather than running 12-TET math under a misleading label.

Expected pitch names:

```text
standard -> E B E G# B E
CGCEGD   -> C A D F  G D
```

Pass conditions:

- carrier digest is identical;
- decoder digests differ;
- projection digests differ;
- changing only reference pitch uniformly rescales frequency while leaving pitch classes fixed.

This is a deterministic foreign-domain control. It does not prove a general theory of meaning.

## `TWO-NARRATOR-001`

One occurrence, two observers, independent exposures and apertures.

Pass conditions:

- the engine keeps one anchored occurrence;
- narrator A may lawfully receive it as memory;
- narrator B may have no observer-visible occurrence at the same cut;
- moving one aperture does not mutate chronology or the other observer's receipt.

## `RUPTURE-REACHABILITY-001`

Record a route from A to F through edge `e`. Append a refused assessment to `e`, then add a separately identified edge `m` that yields another route.

Pass conditions:

- `e` remains in formation history and never returns to an unbroken state;
- any reachable future uses `m` as a new path;
- the topology works without requiring theological vocabulary;
- any theological interpretation remains source-local and is not promoted by the kernel.

## Additional hostile controls

- False-yarn: add an attractive unsupported edge; it must stay unresolved or refused.
- Reinterpretation replay: change a decoder; the prior projection must remain attributable.
- Self-hit: a reinterpretation can inspect its own formation without rewriting the earlier field.
- Future-open: a cut can contain expectations without leaking actual outcomes.
- Boring-field: replace evocative names with neutral identifiers; structural tests must still pass.
- Metaphor removal: remove third-eye, braid, mercy, seed, and gate vocabulary; executable invariants must survive.
